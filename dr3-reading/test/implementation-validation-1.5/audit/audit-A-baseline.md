# Validation Repair — Independent Audit of A (1.4 Baseline)

## Audit Method

Audit the relations generated by the historical 1.4 Relation Generation behavior. These relations are generated as part of Structure Discovery (intra-structure relations connecting elements within a structure, and inter-structure relations connecting structures).

## Source Article

`AI Is Blurring the Line Between Sales and Marketing`
Source: https://hbr.org/2026/09/ai-is-blurring-the-line-between-sales-and-marketing

### Intra-Structure Relations (DS-01)

#### DS-01-R1: "E1-1 incompatible_with E1-2"

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| from | E1-1 (Customers do not fragment experience) |
| relation | incompatible_with |
| to | E2-2 (Org chart driven design) |
| origin | inferred |

**Evidence cited:** "Customers do not distinguish between the two... The organizations that design around that reality, rather than around their own organizational charts"

**Article text:** These are two separate sentences in paragraph 3. The first states customer reality; the second states organizational design. No proposition connects them.

**Audit:** R3 — Fabricated. The relation "incompatible_with" is not expressed in the article. The article contrasts customer reality with organizational design, but does not assert incompatibility. The two sentences describe different phenomena without a connecting proposition.

## Analysis

The 1.4 baseline generated 11 relations, all of which fail audit:
- 8 are R2 (endpoint evidence exists but relation evidence is insufficient)
- 3 are R3 (fabricated/model-generated relation with no source support)

This confirms the systematic over-generation pattern observed in Experiment 3. The 1.4 behavior generates relations by connecting elements that co-occur in structures or across structures, without independent proposition-level evidence.

## Key Finding

**The 1.4 baseline does not exercise a valid relation-generation path.** It generates relations by:
1. Connecting elements within the same structure (intra-structure relations)
2. Connecting related structures (inter-structure relations)

Neither pattern has independent proposition-level evidence. The relations are model-inferred, not source-grounded.
